A Korean War shorts channel took off in Türkiye: why I am adding more accounts on a “just one will hit” plan

Also in: Bahasa Indonesia · हिन्दी · العربية · ไทย

I planned my Korean War channel on a "just one will hit" basis because I could not know which country would take off, and it actually took off in Türkiye. So I decided to make more accounts like this and automate more of them.

It was "just one will hit" from the start

Korea Remembers is a channel about the 22 countries that sent troops and medical teams to the Korean War. I wrote about how I make it in my post on automating with Premiere MCP.

From the planning stage, I assumed I could not know which country the traffic would explode in. So instead of aiming at one country, I built it to place bets on many countries at once. Just one has to hit.

This time it was Türkiye

It went as intended. The traffic did not come in evenly; it exploded in one particular country. This time it was Türkiye.

I do not know where it will be next. This time I saw with my own eyes that laying out many places where something can hit is better than trying to guess where it will hit. After seeing that, I thought I should put out more accounts like this.

What to give the people who came in

The next question is how to keep the viewers who came in through Korean War content. The flow I am thinking about now looks like this.

Stage What happens
Entry Viewers come in through Korean War content
Understanding Look at the gender, age and region of the viewers who came in
Continuing Keep the account going by sharing everyday memes and trending content those people would watch

The Korean War story becomes the entrance, and what comes after is filled with what that country and that age group like. Nothing is decided yet. I am just thinking that maybe this is the way to go.

So more accounts, and more automation

To lay out many places where something can hit, I need many accounts. The more accounts there are, the more of the work I did by hand has to be handed to machines.

So the conclusion I have reached for now is one thing. More accounts, automated more.
