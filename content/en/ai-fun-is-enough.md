We've reached the point where people hit 2.44 million hearts on an AI video, and **my company got on that wave early and is making ads with AI models.**

## The ad model is way too handsome

Lately our team makes ads with AI models.
But every time we make one and look at it, he's way too handsome.

![A scene made with Higgsfield Motion Control: an AI male model raising his index finger as he explains something](/images/20261021-ai-fun-is-enough/01-클링-AI-모델-손가락.webp)

![The same AI male model with his hands together on a table, facing the camera](/images/20261021-ai-fun-is-enough/02-클링-AI-모델-정면.webp)
<small>A 5-second cut made with Higgsfield Motion Control (Kling 3.0). We put an AI-made character over footage of real, filmed movements</small>

Handsome AIs like these are being mass-produced right now.
It means we're not the only ones making them; everyone is. That's why speed matters.

## The mood changed in one year

AI-generated content used to be a bad thing.
People called it something churned out with one click, a devil taking our jobs.
But after a year of that, the mood changed.

| | Past reaction | Reaction now |
|---|---|---|
| Things made by AI | Bad, churned out with one click | Fun is enough |
| Cute AI videos | Creepy | Cute is enough |
| Funny AI videos | Doesn't look real | Funny is enough |
| Comments | Arguing about whether it's AI or not | Comments like that disappeared long ago |

For a short while people rejected AI like a machine-smashing movement, and then they quickly moved over to accepting it.

## Now is YouTube in 2015

I think back to the great YouTube boom of 2015 and 2016.
The people who got established then gained 500,000 or a million subscribers just by standing out a little.

I think AI content is in exactly that spot now.
People no longer reject something because it's AI. If it's fun, they watch.
And all it costs to make is one click.

| When | Situation |
|---|---|
| A year ago | AI was the devil. Make it and you get cursed at |
| Now | Fun is enough. Not everyone has jumped in yet |
| Soon | Everyone clicks. Hard to stand out much |

In [yesterday's post](/posts/content-land-grab/) I said we have to claim our spot before content pours out without limit.
Hearts pouring onto AI content means the starting gun for that race has already fired.

## My company is already running

So my company didn't wait.
While everyone was watching to see whether AI would be accepted, it went into AI ads first, and by making and fixing things quickly it is drawing good responses.

![Editing an AI model ad in Premiere. A vertical video with the caption “Why our company's growth keeps slowing down”](/images/20261021-ai-fun-is-enough/06-프리미어-AI-광고-편집.webp)
<small>Polishing an ad made with an AI model in Premiere. MCP Bridge is connected at the top left, so the AI works Premiere directly</small>

| Trend | What we do |
|---|---|
| AI models are being mass-produced | We make ads with AI models too. We do shoot for real, and put an AI-generated character over that footage |
| People have accepted AI | We don't hold back worrying about pushback; we watch the reaction now and publish right away |
| Trends change by the day | When a new tool comes out, we switch over right away. We also [connected Premiere so the AI can work it directly](/posts/premiere-mcp-setup/) |

You can tell how fast things change just from the tools we use.
Last week we used Higgsfield's Edit Video, and this week we use Motion Control.
There was originally a different menu in Motion Control's spot (I think it was Zenjutsu), and that changed again.

![Higgsfield's video menu. Create Video, Edit Video, Motion Control tabs](/images/20261021-ai-fun-is-enough/07-힉스필드-영상-메뉴.webp)
<small>Edit Video last week, Motion Control this week</small>

Trends change day by day. New tools keep coming out, and video quality keeps getting better.

To pull all this off, your hands have to be fast.
Our team is working at that speed right now. And the company chose to get on board with the change first instead of blocking it.

## Conclusion

The time of rejection was shorter than I expected.
While people were watching to see whether AI would be accepted, it had already been accepted.

Anyone can click.
So **the difference comes not from what you make but from who does it first.**
We are standing on the side that goes first.
