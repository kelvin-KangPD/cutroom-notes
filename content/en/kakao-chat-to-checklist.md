I exported our entire wedding-prep KakaoTalk chat, handed it to Claude, and got a web checklist of what was done and what wasn't that we could tick off together.

There was a huge amount to do before the wedding. Buying long socks, choosing the hanbok, packing for the honeymoon, buying a telescope: countless small things that couldn't be missed. Just making a checklist took ages, and I still missed a lot of things, which got me scolded by my wife quite a bit.

## Why not just hand over the whole chat?

Then it occurred to me: everything we needed to do was already in the conversations with my wife, so why not give them to Claude and ask for a checklist? If we put that checklist on the web, we could tick items off and edit it together. I tried it right away.

From the chat window menu in KakaoTalk, I exported the entire conversation as a text file.

![KakaoTalk PC chat room menu showing Chats, then Export Chats](/images/20261024-kakao-checklist/01-카톡-대화-내보내기.webp)
<small>Chats > Export Chats in the KakaoTalk PC chat room menu</small>

I gave the file to Claude and asked it to sort what we had done and not done from July to the present, group it by category, and build it so it could be edited on the web. It came out well.

## A checklist we tick off together

The finished checklist comes as a Claude link. We both kept that link open, and when one of us ticked an item it showed up on the other's side too. There was a delay of a few seconds, but we could keep updating it almost in real time.

The top section, Remaining, is grouped by date, and each item has a notes field. As the note on the screen says, ticking an item saves automatically and everyone who opens the link sees the same state.

![The Remaining screen: items grouped by date, each with a checkbox and a notes field](/images/20261024-kakao-checklist/02-체크리스트-남은-일.webp)
<small>The Remaining screen. Names and the venue name are hidden</small>

The bottom section, Done, collects the checked items by category such as wedding hall, contracts and payments, outfits, Jeju, vaccinations, and invitations, so you can see everything at a glance.

![The Done screen: completed items grouped by category such as wedding hall, contracts and payments, outfits](/images/20261024-kakao-checklist/03-체크리스트-끝난-일.webp)
<small>The Done screen</small>

As we ticked things off one by one, my wife (my fiancée at the time) stopped worrying about whether we had missed something. Instead she could see right away, oh, this one is still left, and prepare calmly.

That is when I really felt that this is a tool that makes ideas happen. If it's hard to remember, you can just hand it off.
