The idea of claiming the still-empty languages first, **I turned into a site in Indonesian, Hindi and Arabic on the very same day.**

## Just thinking about it is too late

In the [previous post](/en/posts/content-land-grab/) I wrote that I should claim a place in the languages where AI adoption lags, before content floods in endlessly.
The cost of writing and posting dropping close to zero isn't something only I have. If I just keep thinking about it, someone else fills the gap in the meantime.

So I built it the same day.

## The languages I chose, and why

I picked the languages of countries with large populations but little web content.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Site | [Cutroom Notes](/en/), the site you're reading now |
| Languages | Indonesian, Hindi, Arabic (English and Thai were added later) |
| First posts | Four per language. Parts 1–4 of the series "Personal video archive and AI" from my Korean blog |

## AI turns ideas into action

Before, a thought like this would have stayed a thought.
This time I started on the day I had it.

**Being able to start the moment an idea comes.** I think that's where AI is most useful.
