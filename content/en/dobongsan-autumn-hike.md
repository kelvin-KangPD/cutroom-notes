On October 31, the last weekend to see the mountains in autumn colors, my wife and I joined my parents' hike up Dobongsan.

![A selfie with my parents and my wife in front of the rock peaks of Dobongsan](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/01-가족-단체셀카.webp)

## It was supposed to be just my parents

Originally only my mom and dad were going. But my wife was the one who suggested, "Should we go with your parents?" Rain was forecast for the next day, so this was the last week to see the autumn mountains. So we decided to join them.

We left home at 9 a.m. The weather was good. We reached the Dobongsan entrance around 10:20, and since it was Saturday, hikers filled the whole road.

![The crowded entrance to Dobongsan](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/02-도봉산-입구.webp)

## So many foreigners it surprised me

What surprised me most that day was how many foreigners there were. Not just one or two: I think we saw dozens of tour groups. There were lots of young people too. I wondered if Dobongsan has become a must-visit spot for tourists these days.

The foliage started as soon as we entered the trail. Red maples were mixed in among the yellow leaves.

![A Dobongsan forest trail with yellow leaves and red maples](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/03-단풍길.webp)

Around 11:30 we sat on a rock to rest. We shared the sweet potatoes we had packed and drank coffee.

![Sweet potatoes and coffee shared on a rock](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/04-고구마-쉬는시간.webp)

A little higher up, we came across a temple tucked under a huge rock. Hikers were catching their breath there.

![A temple tucked under a huge rock](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/05-바위아래-절.webp)

From a rock near the temple, we could see the city beyond the red maples. My parents stood side by side there and took photos.

![My parents standing side by side in front of red maples](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/06-부모님-단풍전망.webp)

## Majestic rock formations

From around 1 p.m. the trees thinned out and the rocky ridge came into view all at once. The rock formations of Dobongsan were truly majestic. The leaves were beautiful too, and above all, it was good that the four of us went together. The selfie at the top was taken in front of a rock peak around 1:30.

![The rocky ridge and the city seen from high on Dobongsan](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/07-능선-풍경.webp)

## Imone after the hike

We came down around 4 p.m., walked toward the station, and went into a place called Imone.

![In front of Imone, a restaurant near the station](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/09-이모네.webp)

The grilled pork skin was delicious, and the makgeolli after coming down the mountain was refreshingly cold.

![Grilled pork skin at Imone](/images/20261031-dobongsan-autumn-hike/08-하산-안주.webp)
